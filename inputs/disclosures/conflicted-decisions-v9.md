## 1. Why this record exists

Schema Independence Charter v3 §7.6 makes a decision in which the founder's capacities conflict *"a schema-governance act published per Section 6"*. §6 requires that *"Every schema decision, version change, disciplinary-rule application and occupancy-pending null is published to the public version record."* Since 29 September 2026, every founding-period schema decision must be disclosed within five business days of its effective date. Where a decision changes something already relied on externally, it must be disclosed before or at the same time as that reliance (D-DISCLOSURE-INTERVAL, disclosed in v4). When published, this will be the sixth publication run under that rule.

This record is short because its purpose is narrow. Record v8 (published 19 November 2026, release `publish-009`) could not enter the decisions that closed and published it. It disclosed three of them in its status line, and the typed lines of its publication run were disclosed in that release's public log. The founder decided on 23 November which of those need a formal entry. This record makes those entries, and discloses, with five particulars each, the typed lines about the later amendment of that log, which the amended log itself routes to this record. Each line counts as disclosed when public material states its five particulars, including the founder's dated reliance determination of 26 November 2026.

## 2. How to read the entries

The conventions are as v4 §2 states them and v6 §2, v7 §2 and v8 §2 restate them: capacity, inclusion, dates, external reliance (counterparties not named) and review routes (R-§9, R-§5.1, R-V, R-7.4, R-P). R-C: **no solicitor is instructed on these questions.**

**Identifiers and times.** An identifier ends in a suffix that names the working session that issued it. The eight typed lines in Section 3B carry dated labels (`F6-…-20261123`) with no register suffix; each has its own register entry except one (Section 3B line 6; Section 4 item 2). Times are GMT. Where the register gives a time as "about", this record does too.

**The five particulars.** Under AR-20261118-037-944dmr, a covered decision counts as publicly disclosed only when public material states five things about it: its identifier, its date, what was decided, its effect, and whether anyone outside Arkaya relied on it. Section 3B gives those five for each line. The fifth, external reliance, is the founder's determination dated 26 November 2026 (V9-D4-20261126).
