## 1. Why this record exists

Schema Independence Charter v3 §7.6 makes a decision in which the founder's capacities conflict *"a schema-governance act published per Section 6"*. §6 requires that *"Every schema decision, version change, disciplinary-rule application and occupancy-pending null is published to the public version record."* Since 29 September 2026, every founding-period schema decision is disclosed within five business days of its effective date, and earlier where it changes something already relied on externally (D-DISCLOSURE-INTERVAL, disclosed in v4). This is the fourth publication run under that rule.

## 2. How to read the entries

The conventions are as v4 §2 states them and v6 §2 restates them, unchanged: capacity, inclusion, dates, external reliance (counterparties not named) and review routes (R-§9, R-§5.1, R-V, R-7.4, R-P; R-C: **no solicitor is instructed on these questions, and none is planned**).

**Identifiers.** On 3 November 2026 two working sessions each issued identifiers AR-20261103-037 to -050-zcs3uw for different subjects (Section 4 item 3). Under a decision of the same day, every reference to an identifier in that range carries its subject label in brackets, and the identifier alone is not relied on. In this record each such identifier carries its label and source where it appears. The second session's uses are given neutral codes S2-01 to S2-14 in Section 5; their subject matter is not a schema decision and is not disclosed. The S2 codes are this record's own neutral aliases for rows of the reconciliation map; they are not labels assigned by that decision.

**This section is not housekeeping.** It records the places where the disclosure controls did not work as stated. All four items are decided as clause 5 items: item 1 on 3 November; items 2, 3 and 4 on 9 November (AR-20261109-110, -111 and -112-g6nov9).

3. **Two working sessions issued the same identifiers** (Q-13). On 3 November AR-20261103-037 to -050-zcs3uw were issued in two sessions for different subjects. In the first: a counterparty call-note filing, GET amendment package steps and GET steward decisions 1 to 7. The same day (15:54 GMT, AR-20261103-075-zcs3uw (a)) it was decided that no identifier is changed, that every reference carries its subject label, and that the two sessions use separate namespaces from then on. The map of meanings rests on filed attributions, not authentication of either transcript. The second session's use of -050 has no filing receipt; it rests on the reconciliation's account of that session. The first session's meaning of -037 and -039 is not established from files. It is decided to be a clause 5 failure. On 20 October two sessions issued the same numbers under different suffixes, and the suffix distinguished them (v6 §2). In November the full identifier, suffix included, was reused.
4. **A queued decision was left out of the queue** (PUB-7). The decision record approving v6's publication queues that approval for the record after v6.
