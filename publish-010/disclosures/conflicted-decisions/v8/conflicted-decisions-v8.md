## 1. Why this record exists

Schema Independence Charter v3 §7.6 makes a decision in which the founder's capacities conflict *"a schema-governance act published per Section 6"*. §6 requires that *"Every schema decision, version change, disciplinary-rule application and occupancy-pending null is published to the public version record."* Since 29 September 2026, every founding-period schema decision must be disclosed within five business days of its effective date. Where a decision changes something already relied on externally, it must be disclosed before or at the same time as that reliance (D-DISCLOSURE-INTERVAL, disclosed in v4). When published, this will be the fifth publication run under that rule.

## 2. How to read the entries

The conventions are as v4 §2 states them and v6 §2 and v7 §2 restate them: capacity, inclusion, dates, external reliance (counterparties not named) and review routes (R-§9, R-§5.1, R-V, R-7.4, R-P). R-C: **no solicitor is instructed on these questions.** Section 6 item 6 records one question on which counsel is recorded as needed before any notice is given.

**One row for each ruling.** Where one approval carried several rulings, each ruling has its own row, with its own designation and typed time. Rows 1, 2, 47 and 48 are groups, as the decisions that admitted them direct.

**Identifiers and times.** An identifier ends in a suffix that names the working session that issued it. Fourteen decisions of 17 November were taken under short labels (SC2, RA-D1 and so on) with no register identifier. Each is cited by its label, its typed time and its decision record (rows 22 to 35; Section 4 item 6). One entry has no identifier and is cited by release and stop point (row 3). Times are GMT.

**The export-binding contract.** Rows 7 to 11, 14 to 21 and 36 to 42 concern a working draft that sets out how a GET export is tied to the sealed records it is drawn from, and how a reader checks that tie. It is intended for GET 1.1. It is experimental and it is not issued. A ruling on it settles a rule of the draft, or of one round of testing where the row says so. No ruling adopts the contract or changes GET v6. Words in code type are the draft's own result codes and member names.
